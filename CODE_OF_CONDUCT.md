# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Accepting feedback gracefully
- Prioritizing collaboration over ego

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of
acceptable behavior and may take appropriate corrective action for unacceptable
behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, and other communication channels.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior through the process in `SECURITY.md` or by
contacting maintainers.
